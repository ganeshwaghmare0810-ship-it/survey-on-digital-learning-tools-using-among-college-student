## 1. Slide-by-Slide Walkthrough & Speaker Notes

### Slide 1: Title & Candidacy
- **Header:** B.Sc. (Data Science) · Field Project Report
- **Title:** Survey on Digital Learning Tools Usage Among College Students
- **Candidate:** Waghmare Ganesh Jalindar (PRN: 81) | Guide: Prof. Arjana Shaikh
- **Speaker Note:** *"Good morning respected evaluators. Today I present my empirical field project investigating the adoption, academic utility, and satisfaction drivers of digital learning tools among college students."*

### Slide 2: Introduction — The Digital Shift in Higher Education
- **Content:** Everyday educational reality; reliance on video tutorials, LMS platforms, and generative AI.
- **Key Message:** Education has transitioned from textbook-exclusive paradigms to hybrid self-directed inquiry.

### Slide 3: Research Design — Aim & Core Objectives
- **Content:** Six formal research questions; study aim: evaluate usage patterns, productivity impacts, and satisfaction drivers.

### Slide 4: Methodology & Sampling Framework
- **Content:** Descriptive survey design, Google Form primary collection (Aug 21 – Sept 7, 2026), $N = 69$ validated responses, Python & Power BI toolchain.

### Slide 5: Demographic Profile (6.1)
- **Visuals:** Age histogram (Mean: 19.93, Range: 18–26) and Gender pie chart (66.7% Male, 31.9% Female, 1.4% Other).
- **Course Distribution:** Heavy concentration in SY/TY Data Science and Computer Science tracks.

### Slide 6: Engagement & Study Duration (6.2)
- **Visuals:** Tool Adoption (94.2% Yes) and Frequency Distribution (79.7% daily/near-daily).
- **Daily Time:** 62.3% spend 30 minutes to 2 hours per day on digital study.

### Slide 7: Platform Ecosystem & Utility (6.3)
- **Visuals:** Multi-select tools breakdown. YouTube and ChatGPT tie for #1 (68.1% each).
- **Most Useful Tool:** ChatGPT leads decisively with 79.7% of all votes.

### Slide 8: Helpfulness, Benefits & Core Advantages (6.4 & 6.5)
- **Visuals:** 71.0% find tools Very/Extremely helpful; 30.4% cite better understanding of difficult topics.
- **Biggest Advantage:** All-round benefits (33.3%) and instant access to information (26.1%).

### Slide 9: Devices, Locations & Friction Points (6.5 & 6.6)
- **Visuals:** Smartphone primacy (53.6%), Home access (55.1%).
- **Hurdles:** Paid/limited features (59.4%) and internet connectivity (24.6%).

### Slide 10: Economics & Generative AI Academic Workflows (6.6)
- **Visuals:** Cost perception split (52.2% see digital as costlier); AI purposes: 40.6% clear doubts, 37.7% coding/debugging.

### Slide 11: Statistical Driver Analysis (6.7)
- **Visuals:** Frequency vs. Helpfulness crosstab & Pearson Correlation Heatmap.
- **Key Insight:** Information Trust $\leftrightarrow$ Overall Experience ($r = +0.686$), Usage Frequency $\leftrightarrow$ Experience ($r = +0.533$).

### Slide 12: Key Findings at a Glance
- **Content:** Summary card matrix of core metrics (94.2% adoption, 79.7% daily engagement, 82.6% satisfaction).

### Slide 13: Conclusions & Recommendations
- **Content:** Dual pillars of digital study: YouTube + ChatGPT; institutional software licensing and campus Wi-Fi expansion.

### Slide 14: Academic Reflection & Study Limitations
- **Content:** Convenience sampling limitations, disciplinary focus, self-reported metrics.

### Slide 15: Q&A / Defense Conclusion
- **Content:** Candidate contact details, institutional affiliations, thank you note.

## 2. Anticipated Viva Defense Questions & Answers

| Viva Question | Recommended Strategic Response |
|:---|:---|
| **Q1: Why did you drop the 'name' column in your analysis?** | *"To adhere strictly to research ethics, data privacy principles (GDPR), and PII anonymization. Academic survey evaluations require behavioral insights, not individual personal identification."* |
| **Q2: Why do you think YouTube and ChatGPT tied for #1?** | *"They serve complementary cognitive roles. YouTube provides asynchronous visual lecture walkthroughs, whereas ChatGPT functions as an interactive, real-time 1-on-1 tutor for instant debugging and doubt clearing."* |
| **Q3: What does the correlation coefficient of 0.686 between Trust and Experience signify?** | *"It is a strong positive Pearson correlation indicating that a student's satisfaction with digital tools is directly governed by their confidence in the accuracy and truthfulness of the content, highlighting the danger of AI hallucinations."* |
| **Q4: Why do 52.2% of students believe digital learning is more expensive?** | *"While digital content is often marketed as free, students incur real peripheral expenses: smartphone/laptop hardware depreciation, recurring mobile high-speed data packs, and paywalled advanced features."* |
| **Q5: If you had more time, how would you expand this study?** | *"I would expand the sample size across non-STEM disciplines (Arts, Commerce) and implement longitudinal tracking to measure objective GPA correlations before and after digital tool adoption."* |
